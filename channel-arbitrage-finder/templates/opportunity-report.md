## Opportunity · repeat this section for each finalist

### The gap

Write one plain sentence about what viewers want, what current creators leave underserved and why the window may be open now. Identify the strategy (better execution, transfer, or creator scale), confidence, evidence against the idea, and what could overturn it. Mark an unverified gap as a hypothesis.

### Observed evidence

Give 3–5 linked channels or videos per finalist, including ordinary or contrary observations. If fewer exist, report the shortfall. For transfer, distinguish proof of origin from destination evidence. Log the exact searches, independent creators versus videos inspected, visible upload cadence and any changes in supply or comparable views per video. If an account field is unavailable use “unverified.” Carry rounded public numbers through the calculation.

### Channel concept

Give three original name candidates, one sentence of positioning, the precise target viewer, and a publishable channel description. Names are creative suggestions. State the recurring question, how each episode proves its answer and what makes the hundredth episode possible.

### Content and packaging

Name three content pillars. Write ten specific titles, each labelled **adapted from a proven outlier** with the source ID, or **new angle** with its hypothesis. For an idea informed only by a precedent, label it **new angle, inspired by** that source. Deliver three thumbnail concepts with subject, composition, minimal on-image words and the question the image opens.

### Record the first episode

Write the first 15 seconds verbatim. Supply the target length, an ordered timed outline with the reveal and a single spoken call to action consistent with the user's goal. Specify the first asset, prop or demonstration the creator needs this week.

### Art direction and format

Apply the confirmed production branch. For faceless + AI, supply a named style, palette, three complete prompts (opening, explanation, payoff), voice direction and a priced tool stack with limits. For faceless human-made, give footage sources and rights, capture/animation, narration and edit choices. For on-camera, give shot setup, framing, lights, audio and studio routine using owned gear, delivery and B-roll. For hybrid, assign the actual segments to each mode, give the relevant implementation details and explain the transitions.

### Production and monetisation

List each step from idea to upload with minutes, owner and cash cost. Show totals per video, first-month cash, hours/week and what is already owned. Distinguish budget cap from actual price. Provide niche RPM range and source or “unverified,” plus sponsor and product fit with evidence or explicit assumptions. Explain what must happen for the user's primary goal to be met.

### Thirty-day launch test

Give the number of uploads and time/cost for **this opportunity alone**. Show a weekly schedule, one variable to change at a time, the comparison baseline, metrics and a decision on day 30. Include thumbnail impressions click-through rate where available, retention at hook/reveal, views/subscriber where defined, and a goal metric. State when low reach leaves the test inconclusive. Provide a continue, revise and stop condition; a ceiling on available hours is a real constraint.

### Risks and responses

Pair each material risk with a feasible action. Cover rights, accuracy and policy, production constraints, supply growth and the gap closing. For a gap unsupported by sufficient public evidence, name the specific proof still needed.

## Remaining candidates

Give one reason for dropping each non-finalist. Connect it to the creator profile or an identified market observation. Close the report with a single question.

**Which opportunity would you like to develop further?**
