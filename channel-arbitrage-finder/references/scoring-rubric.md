# Ranking a shortlist

**Addition A3.** Victor defines the gap and names repeated small-account breakouts as its signal. These weights and anchors make decisions comparable. They express research judgment, not a predicted chance of success. Read the [Method Brief](method.md) and [research playbook](research-playbook.md) before assigning scores.

First exclude refused topics, infeasible equipment or time, unavailable essential rights, or a required face when the user declines to appear. If an original, affordable adjustment changes the format, research the adjusted format afresh. Keep a one-line reason for every exclusion. Then use an integer from 1 to 10 for each remaining candidate.

| Dimension | Weight | 1 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Gap size | 30% | Demand or supply unknown, or strong comparable supply already satisfies viewers | Some demand and a specific plausible weakness, with mixed competition | Repeated demand plus multiple corroborated shortcomings or scarcity of competent supply in the target cohort |
| Proof | 20% | No verified recent qualifying outlier, or evidence unknown | Two recent breakouts on one account, or incomplete independent replication | Several recent breakouts across independent small accounts, backed by ordinary comparisons and reliable counts |
| Creator fit | 15% | Weak interest, credibility or language match | Interest and language fit, limited direct knowledge | Sustained interest, expertise/access and audience-language match, with many feasible episodes |
| Production fit | 15% | Cannot execute within confirmed resources | Feasible after narrowing scope, thin time or cost buffer | Existing gear and skills, room for reshoots and a repeatable cadence |
| Monetisation | 8% | Unsupported or no credible route to stated goal | A plausible buyer or revenue path with limited direct evidence | Comparable, sourced monetisation or an existing offer that matches this viewer and creator |
| Durability | 7% | Closing window or little visibility into trend | Some defensibility with uncertain supply trend | Comparable periods and access barriers support a sustained advantage for this creator |
| Risk, inverted | 5% | High unresolved rights, policy, claims or execution exposure | Manageable risks with specific controls | Original assets, verifiable claims, clear permissions and limited residual exposure |

Interpolate between anchors and cite the observation or profile fact that moves a score. For unknown gap or proof use 1 and state what is missing. Distinguish a control that reduces a risk from evidence that a risk never existed.

**Weighted total out of 10** = (30 × gap + 20 × proof + 15 × creator fit + 15 × production fit + 8 × monetisation + 7 × durability + 5 × inverted risk) / 100. For 7, 6, 9, 8, 5, 4, 7, the result is 6.88. Round only the final result to two decimals.

**Evidence status precedes total.** “Qualified to test” requires repeated target-market small-account outliers under the declared screen, evidence of limited or weak comparable supply, and feasible production. “Pilot only” means a credible, feasible creative idea with missing proof or a still-unverified gap. “Drop” means infeasible, disallowed or less compelling than the finalists. Supply under-sampling, historical evidence or weak destination proof cannot be cured by a high planning score. Use a lower score where evidence is contrary. Report the missing observation separately from negative findings.

Rank qualified candidates first, followed by pilots. Break equal scores by stronger gap evidence, then independent replication, then lower first-month burden. Keep the best three feasible concepts across those tiers and give a specific drop reason for all others. A shortlist containing only pilots is a valid research result. State that the month is for testing and say what would justify a larger commitment.
