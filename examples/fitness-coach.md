# Test B · An on-camera fitness coach with a home studio

**First choice: test standing, no-repeat dumbbell strength.** Four independent channels under 100,000 subscribers drew 2.56–12.61 times their following on recent full-body episodes. A floor-free session with spoken cues and quiet transitions is an inexpensive first shoot. The public evidence establishes appetite for the underlying format. It has yet to establish a shortage of competent coaches who deliver those extras. All three plans below are **pilot only**.

Big numbers alone would not justify launching three months of episodes.

| Time | Shot and action |
|---|---|

**Art direction and studio.** Put the phone horizontally on the tripod at waist height. Mark the place where hands overhead and feet fit within the frame, then check it from the phone. Put the soft light 45° to the front, window on the opposite side, and record ten seconds with the lav clipped away from moving weights. Keep the wide master through every transition. Film B-roll of a controlled set-down, a foot crossing the boundary and the next hand position, then use the useful inserts only during rests. Say each exercise name before the first rep and pause after giving the cue. A dark background and pale-yellow timer keep feet and timer readable.
