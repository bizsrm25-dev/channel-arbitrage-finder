## Production budget and decision after 30 days

Choose **one** of the three four-upload plans. The suggested US stack is Adobe Firefly Standard for backgrounds and props ($9.99/month), ElevenLabs Starter for commercially licensed synthetic narration (budget the recurring $6/month), and free DaVinci Resolve for diagram overlays, motion, assembly and captions. For four uploads the recurring budget is $15.99, or $4.00 each rounded, before tax. The listed $1 first-month ElevenLabs offer would make month one $10.99, about $2.75 per upload if eligible. Firefly Standard lists 2,000 monthly credits, and ElevenLabs Starter lists 30,000.
