## Verification and limits

Both sample runs screened ten candidates. Each produced three complete pilot plans, thirty episode titles, nine thumbnail concepts and three first-episode hooks. Four recent standing dumbbell classes on independent small accounts passed the demand screen. The supply advantage of the proposed coaching treatment remains unverified. The calculator, slide-rule and diskette samples also fall short of confirming their proposed long-form gaps. The [public research ledger](research-ledger.md) preserves the query-level evidence and prior-period checks.

The source video was reviewed with the installed Watch skill's local frame workflow and full-track on-device transcription. The original lecture supplies no fixed outlier cutoff, channel-age threshold, RPM band or dated research window. The pack labels its screening settings as author additions.

The ZIP's ten members match the source folder byte for byte, and the ten fenced files match those same members. The skill passed the bundled skill validator. The install guide uses the documented locations for Codex and Claude Code and the documented ZIP-upload flow for Claude.ai. The pack was exercised against two simulated profiles with live public research. Import into a separate Claude.ai account and publication of pilot videos were outside these tests.

Public before-and-after subscriber counts, competitors' private retention and comparable niche earnings remain unverified where the sources do not expose them. A format can show repeated breakouts and still lack evidence of an underserved audience. The pilot label keeps that distinction visible.
